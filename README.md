# MSN — Finance Dashboard

### Finance, explained the way a friend would explain it.

An interactive **Finance Fundamentals Dashboard** built to make financial concepts easier to understand, practice and revise.

I built this while studying finance as an MBA candidate interested in **Wealth Management & Investment Advisory**. Instead of keeping my notes as static formulas and textbook definitions, I turned them into an interactive learning tool.

---

## What is this?

MSN Finance Dashboard is a single-page, browser-based finance learning resource covering the fundamentals of accounting, financial management, financial analysis, valuation and portfolio risk.

The idea is simple:

**Plain English → Formula → Example → Interactive Calculator**

No frameworks. No complicated setup. Just open the HTML file and start learning.

## The mental model

The dashboard is designed around five simple questions:

| Question | What to look at |
|---|---|
| Did the business make money? | P&L |
| What does it own and owe? | Balance Sheet |
| Did cash actually move? | Cash Flow |
| How good is the performance? | Financial Ratios |
| How is the market pricing it? | Valuation & Risk |

The goal is to understand **how the pieces connect**, rather than memorising isolated formulas.

## Learning approach

This project follows a simple philosophy:

> **Don't just memorise the formula. Understand what the number is trying to tell you.**

A ratio is useful only when you understand the business question behind it.

For example:

**Current Ratio**

```text
Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities
```

isn't just a formula to memorise.

It answers:

> **Can the business cover its short-term obligations with its short-term assets?**

That is the approach used throughout the dashboard.
